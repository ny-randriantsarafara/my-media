# Presentation mockups

Discussion aids created on 10 October 2026. Name and topic are still open; proposal 2 of the minimal review is the V1 base.

## Proposed directions

- **A — Sober newspaper**: off-white background, reading typography, clear hierarchy, images beside text.
- **B — Visual magazine**: large opening image, strong headlines, articles in cards.
- **C — Minimal review**: more whitespace, calmer layout, secondary articles as a list.

The owner chose C and asked for a less generic look inspired by major French and international news sites, with higher quality ambition. The first comparison is kept as history; the new proposal refines direction C.

## Editorial review, proposal 2

Displayed version: `content/revue-editoriale-v4.html`. Screenshot: `state/revue-editoriale-accueil.png`.

- Full-width home: one lead story with text and photo, a secondary selection, then a chronological list.
- Near-white background, serif headlines, quiet sections, thin dividers.
- Photos by Wyatt Simpson and Annie Spratt from identified Unsplash pages. No press photos copied from reference news sites.
- Article page with demo text, reference links, and a “Sources and documents” block near the byline.
- Example sourced from a real Insee publication; other headlines and identity are demo content.
- Home and article page, both photos loading, and mobile at 390 px were checked. The sources link sits under the date on mobile.

Reference research and layout choices are in [Editorial references](references-editoriales.md). The owner accepted this proposal for now: V1 base, open to change. It is not the product implementation.
